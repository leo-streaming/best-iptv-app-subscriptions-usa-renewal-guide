## 1. What Are IPTV App Subscriptions?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It describes television and video delivery using internet protocols rather than traditional broadcast, cable, or satellite distribution.

An IPTV app subscription can refer to a paid media player, access to a streaming service, or a combination of both.

Understanding this distinction is essential before purchasing.

### What Is an IPTV Player?

An IPTV player is an application that organizes and plays compatible media streams.

Depending on its design, a player may provide:

- Electronic Program Guide (EPG) integration
- Channel favorites and categories
- Search and navigation tools
- Playback controls
- Playlist management
- Multiple user profiles
- Parental-control functions
- Accessibility settings
- Customizable viewing interfaces

A paid IPTV player does not necessarily provide television channels, movies, or other content.

### What Is an IPTV Service Subscription?

An IPTV service subscription provides access to a streaming provider's content or services under its specific terms.

The subscription may require a separate application, account credentials, a supported device, or compatible playback software.

Content availability, simultaneous viewing, service regions, and usage rights depend on the provider's documented policies.

### What Is an IPTV App Store Subscription?

An app-store subscription is an eligible recurring purchase processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play.

The platform provides subscription-management controls for purchases billed through its system.

However, downloading an app through an official store does not automatically mean the associated streaming subscription is billed by that store.

### What Is Direct IPTV Provider Billing?

Direct provider billing occurs when the streaming provider or its payment processor manages the purchase outside Apple's or Google's subscription systems.

The provider's billing portal, receipt, or written service terms should identify the applicable renewal and cancellation process.

## 2. IPTV Billing Terminology: Essential Definitions

Understanding common subscription terminology helps consumers interpret checkout pages accurately.

### Automatic Renewal

Automatic renewal means a subscription is scheduled to continue into another billing period unless canceled or otherwise changed under the applicable terms.

### Billing Cycle

A billing cycle is the recurring period for which the customer is charged, such as monthly or annually.

### Renewal Date

The renewal date is when the next subscription billing period is expected to begin or a new recurring charge is scheduled.

### Subscription Expiration

Expiration refers to the scheduled end of access or a subscription term. A canceled subscription may remain usable until its paid period ends.

### Introductory Offer

An introductory offer is a promotional arrangement that may have different initial and subsequent prices.

### Trial-to-Paid Conversion

Trial-to-paid conversion occurs when an eligible trial changes into a paid subscription under disclosed conditions.

Not every trial converts automatically.

### Subscription Cancellation

Cancellation is the process of ending recurring renewal or terminating the applicable subscription agreement.

Its effect on existing access depends on the billing terms.

### Refund

A refund is a repayment of an eligible charge. Canceling a subscription does not automatically guarantee a refund.

### Billing Platform

The billing platform is the company or system responsible for processing and managing the purchase.

### Electronic Program Guide (EPG)

An EPG displays program information and schedules within compatible television applications.

### Concurrent Streams

Concurrent streams are the number of viewing sessions a service permits simultaneously.

The number of devices on which an app can be installed may differ from the number of permitted simultaneous streams.
